Do / Don't

✅ Do

  • 2.5–3px ink outlines + hard offset shadow (4px 4px 0 ink) on every card
  • Let colour carry the meaning: mint = safe, red = danger, never decorative
  • Piggy reacts to everything — he's the emotional meter
  • All on-screen text (SMS, chats, warrants) in HTML, never baked into video
  • Spring easing for UI, slow push-in for media

🚫 Don't

  • No real bank / police / app logos or real people (course ethics rule)
  • No glassmorphism, no purple-blue "AI startup" gradients on the UI chrome
  • No more than 2 choices per node — speed matters more than options
  • Don't make bad endings preachy — show consequence, then offer rewind
  • No auto-playing sound before the first click
